Double Calendar Spread — Strategy Builder

Sell a near-term call + put (a little OTM each side), buy the same two strikes in a far-term expiry. Net DEBIT, defined risk (max loss = debit paid), long vega / positive theta. Profit is maximized if price sits between the two short strikes ("sweet spot") when the near leg expires. This tool auto-picks both expiries and both strikes for you — override either if you want manual control.

⚡ Auto Trade Mode — status

This is a rule-based intraday breakout scanner + option buyer — not a guaranteed-profit system. It watches your configured symbols for a price breakout above/below their recent trading range, buys the ATM option in that direction, and manages stop-loss/target/trailing-stop itself. In Manual mode nothing is ever sent to your broker until you click "Execute". In Auto mode it places that entry the moment a signal qualifies — real money, no click. Only arm Auto mode with capital you can afford to lose, and keep an eye on your Zerodha app.
Not armed
"Start scanning" arms the continuous background scanner (it keeps running and re-scanning every poll interval until you click Stop). "Manual" shows you each signal and waits for you to click Execute; "Auto" places the entry itself the instant a signal qualifies — no click. Either way, the "Scan now" button further down is separate: it's a one-off, no-commitment preview scan you can run any time, armed or not, to see current candidates without starting anything.
Stop-loss / target / trailing-stop management and auto-close apply to any open position — whether it came from clicking Execute in Manual mode or from Auto mode — and keep running in the background even after you click "Stop scanning". Stop scanning only stops NEW entries from being taken; it does not stop watching a position you already have open. Use the kill switch to force an immediate exit instead.

Settings

Scans every live F&O stock (~180-220 names) instead of a hand-picked list. Kite's historical-data API is rate-limited (~3 requests/sec), so the full list is scanned in rotating batches of 40 a cycle — it fully cycles through every name every few minutes rather than all at once. While this is on, the background poll interval is floored at 45s regardless of the setting below, and a manual "Scan now" click does a single full pass (can take up to ~a minute).
Stock options are usually monthly-expiry only (indices also get weekly) and can be far less liquid than NIFTY/BANKNIFTY — wider bid-ask spreads mean your real fill may be noticeably worse than the LTP shown. Consider a smaller budget per trade for stocks until you've watched a few go through.
